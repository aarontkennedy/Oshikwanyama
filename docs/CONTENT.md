Source: *Hai ti! A Beginner's Guide to Oshikwanyama*, © 2004 Thera Crane, Karl
Lindgren-Streicher and Andy Wingo, CC BY-SA 2.0 (once at schoolnet.na/language). The

## Transcription

The text was taken from the book's PDF and checked against page images wherever the PDF's
text came out in the wrong order (tables, two-column exercises, the Grammar Reference).
Oshikwanyama spellings are left exactly as printed, including ones that vary between pages
(for example *omulongi ouliyambi* in the chapter 3 dialogue and *omulongi omuliyambi* in its
word list), since they may be dialect or the authors' choice.

## Changes from the book

- **Layout**: the book's pages are reflowed for a phone. Two-column exercises are a single
  list; tables that are too wide scroll on their own.
- **Exercises** are collapsed "Practice" boxes. The book's answer key was online only and is
  not included, so exercises aren't marked.
- **Drawings**: the PDF's drawings (chapters 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11) are blank, so the "label the
  drawing" exercises (chapter 8 exercise 1, chapter 10 exercise 1, chapter 11 exercise 1) are
  left out.
- **Speech-bubble page** at the start of chapter 2 is left out; the same phrases are in its
  word list.
- **Small wording changes**, each to fit the format:
  - Word lists always read English, then Oshikwanyama (the adjectives table in chapter 10 is
    headed "Oshindonga" in the book).
  - "Iron" is marked (noun) and (verb) in chapter 10, where the book lists it twice.
  - Chapter 10's adjective prefixes that the book shows in italics are marked with *.
  - Chapter 9 gathers the Telling Time examples into a list after the text that gives them.
  - Chapter 12's Further Reading notes that the list dates from 2004.
  - Chapter 6 exercise 2 has no instruction in the book, and none here.
  - Chapter 8 exercise 5 is a list rather than a table to fill in.
- **English summaries** of each chapter's title (such as "Greetings" for *Wa lele po?*) are
  the app's.
- **Quizzes** are the app's: they're made from the book's word lists and tables, so every
  answer is the book's.
